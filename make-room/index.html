Your progress

Progress is saved on your device. Use the in-game progress-backup options to save or restore a copy. Keep exported backups safe: clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress. Restore an ad-removal purchase through Settings using the same Google Play account.

Ads and purchases

The planned release is free to download, with occasional between-level ads and optional videos for extra hints. A one-time purchase removes automatic ads; optional rewarded videos remain available when you choose them. Google Play shows the local price before checkout. No subscription is required.

Refunds and purchase support

Google Play processes purchases and refund requests. We offer refunds for verified accidental or duplicate purchases and when ad removal does not work and we cannot resolve the problem. Email jackwillia141@gmail.com with your Google Play order ID and a short explanation. Do not send card details or passwords. You can also request a refund through Google Play; Google's eligibility rules apply to requests handled by Google. This policy does not limit your statutory consumer rights.

A refunded and revoked ad-removal purchase no longer removes automatic ads. Your puzzle progress is separate from purchase ownership. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can erase local progress, so export a backup before doing either.
