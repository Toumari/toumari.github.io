MAKE ROOM / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026. This policy covers Make Room by Toumari, Android package com.toumari.parcelplatform.makeroom.

Who is responsible

Toumari develops Make Room. Contact jackwillia141@gmail.com for support or privacy requests. This policy describes the game and its integrated Google advertising, consent and payment services. Their availability may differ between development and released versions.

Your progress and settings

The game stores puzzle progress, moves and Undo history, completed levels, stars, postcards, daily deliveries, hint availability, sound and haptic preferences, and cached purchase ownership in the app's private storage on your device. Local playtest counters record actions such as starts, hints and retries. Toumari does not upload these game records to a developer-operated server. The game has no Toumari sign-in or cloud-save service. From version 1.1.1, optional gameplay measurement is provided by Google Firebase Analytics as described below.

If you choose to export a progress backup, Android lets you select where to save the file. A cloud provider you choose may receive and retain it under its own policy. You control copies of those backups. Purchase ownership is not included in progress exports.

Advertising and consent

Make Room integrates Google AdMob for occasional automatic ads between ordinary completed puzzles and optional videos for extra hints. A one-time purchase removes automatic ads; optional rewarded videos remain available when you choose them.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks consent requirements before ad requests. Where required, you can accept or reject available advertising purposes. Necessary automatic-ad consent forms are presented on Home; requesting an optional video may also require a consent choice. Where available, open the ad-removal section in Settings and select Privacy choices to review your choices. Declining consent does not block the core puzzle game.

When advertising services are used, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share your IP address, which can indicate approximate location; device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID; product interactions such as app launches, taps and video views; and diagnostic/performance information. Google uses these for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This applies to integrated services even though Toumari does not operate a game-data server.

The game does not request precise GPS location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access. Advertising identifiers may be reset or deleted through Android's advertising settings. Google may process information outside your country under its applicable privacy safeguards.

Read Google's privacy policy, Google's information about partner apps and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosures for details.

Optional gameplay analytics (version 1.1.1 and later)

You can choose whether to share gameplay statistics with Google Analytics for Firebase. Collection is off until you choose Share statistics. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Gameplay statistics. Declining or withdrawing this choice does not affect puzzles, hints, progress or your advertising choices.

When enabled, Google receives a random app-installation identifier, app version and device information, approximate country/region inferred from network information, installation/acquisition and session information, screen visits, and selected gameplay events such as puzzle starts and completions, slides, stars, time spent, hints, retries, Undo, line clears and ad or purchase-flow outcomes. This lets Toumari understand acquisition, usage, retention, difficulty and monetisation through Firebase/Google Analytics reports. We do not send names, email addresses, precise GPS locations, puzzle layouts, saved-game files or support messages to Analytics. Analytics advertising-ID collection, advertising personalization and advertising data-use signals are disabled in this integration; AdMob has separate consent controls.

Withdrawing consent disables future Analytics collection and resets the app’s local Analytics identifier/data; it does not erase your game progress or automatically erase events already received by Google. Contact us about access or deletion requests concerning analytics data. Google may process data outside your country under its safeguards. See Firebase privacy information and how Google uses information from partner apps. Earlier versions do not include this optional Analytics SDK.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes checkout and payments. Toumari does not receive your card number or payment credentials. The app receives purchase status and signed purchase information to verify and restore your non-consumable ad-removal purchase. It stores an ownership flag locally and checks ownership with Google Play. Google keeps purchase and transaction records under its policies and applicable legal requirements. Pending or cancelled payments do not unlock the purchase.

Support messages

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you send to respond, investigate the issue and handle your request. Please include only what is needed, such as your app version and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve and follow up your request or meet applicable legal obligations; you can request its deletion.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Exported backup files remain wherever you saved them until you delete those copies. Clearing local data does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase; ownership can be restored with the same Google Play account.

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information held by Toumari. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, withdraw consent or complain to your data-protection authority. Requests concerning information controlled by Google should use Google's linked privacy resources. Google's advertising and payment services have their own retention rules; deleting local game data does not automatically delete their records.

Security

Game records use Android's app-private storage. Google states that data transmitted by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No system can promise absolute security. Keep your device and any exported backups secure.

Intended audience

Make Room's V1 is designed and marketed for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children. This is its intended audience, not a claim that the puzzle game contains adult material. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Toumari, contact us so we can address it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Toumari has added no advertising scripts, analytics or tracking cookies to this page. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure hosting. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when the game's data practices change. Where a new choice is required, it will be requested in the app.
